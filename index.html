The project

Cultural heritage is universal. Access should be too.

VIA Project explores the accessibility challenges of two historic European routes, seeking to understand barriers and identify opportunities for inclusive access while respecting cultural heritage.

Research territories

Two routes. One common challenge.

Spain

Camino de Santiago

Exploring accessibility along historic pilgrimage routes, from path surfaces to information and services.

Italy

Via Appia

Investigating inclusive access to ancient infrastructure while respecting its archaeological significance.

Proposed research approach

From barriers to solutions.

  1. Research

    Understand needs and barriers.

  2. Mapping

    Document conditions and obstacles.

  3. Co-creation

    Involve people with disabilities in design.

  4. Innovation

    Develop and evaluate potential improvements.

Designing for everyone

Nothing about us without us.

People with disabilities should be active contributors to research, design and evaluation.
